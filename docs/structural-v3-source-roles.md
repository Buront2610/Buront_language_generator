# B3: source-role binding before rhetorical realization

B3 is a bounded development iteration over the 203 already exposed B1/B2 inputs. It is not an unseen evaluation, a human style judgment, or a claim that the generator has solved ordinary Japanese. B2 remains a separate immutable checkpoint; B3 programs and relations are version 3 and do not silently reinterpret version-2 locks.

## Diagnosis

B2's ordinary-generalization failure was not just missing surface phrases. A small completed-verb vocabulary and a small assessment-topic vocabulary licensed relations before their proposition roles were resolved. Flat safety gates treated every predicate under an assessment or evidential object as an independent assertion. Parser details compounded the problem: nominalized `csubj` predicates were absent from the scope inventory, the connective `それでも` inherited deictic ambiguity from its first token, and compound/potential polite forms could be lemmatized incorrectly.

B3 retains the pipeline: immutable source propositions and illocution → typed source-grounded operators → source-slice clause/document program with separately licensed local edits → independent source-rebound verification. It does not replace this with sentence-specific output templates or a looser pass gate.

## Role contracts

Each rhetorical condition now records a `sourceRole`: assessment content, asserted event, embedded description, causal proposition, request shell, mentioned target, literal context, or relation marker. The underlying fact extractor's tense, polarity, voice and realization remain recorded rather than overwritten to fit an operator.

- A self-assessment binds its entire original scope. Nested activities, degree judgments, conditionals and negated `わけ` descriptions remain mentioned/literal assessment material. An unrelated negative token or a competence word inside someone else's report does not license modest self-presentation
- Completed-event binding uses the source's positive past/perfective morphology, asserted matrix role and actor boundaries. It has no completed-verb whitelist and adds no claim of success, praise or rank. The legacy `achievementKind` field distinguishes completed events, explicit past ability, perfective result state, and past events whose lexical modality is unresolved
- Independent matrix/coordinated actors must remain first-person or omitted. Actors in relative or nominalized descriptions never become the matrix actor. An unresolved generic-noun evaluand, non-first-person owner or dative assessment target is a refusal, even when the text could have a benign reading. A wrapper narrator does not authorize a different embedded evaluand. Direct first-person evaluators, parser-typed action nominals and grammatical nominalizations provide bounded recovery without a topic/person vocabulary
- Nested judgments and nominalized activities inside causal clauses are mentioned, scope-preserved material. Copying `彼が犯人だと判断するのは早すぎた` does not certify `彼が犯人だ` as an asserted fact
- Requested evidence carriers have a literal object, exact requested action, nested target reference, and source markers. Carrier names are open vocabulary; their evidential role comes from a dependency-connected proof/judgment/verification relation. Presentation, reading and inspection permission retain the requested action

## Composition and grammar

Single-sentence assessment concessions can be split into assessment followed by the source event. Two-sentence assessment/concession blocks can be fused while retaining the original discourse marker. The inserted `とはいえ` and fusion linkage are explicitly ordinary grammar primitives serving the source-attested assessment/event operation, not newly claimed corpus quotations. These outputs can be structurally different while still stylistically ordinary.

Trailing `からだ` and unambiguous causal `ためだ` explanations can be brought before the original bounded claim. Future, conditional and potential claims retain their wording/modality; nonpast verbal claims conservatively retain unresolved temporal commitment even without a recognized future-time word and cannot use fact-nominalization recipes. Purpose `ために` remains unsupported when causality is ambiguous.

Grammar repairs cover compound continuative predicates with auxiliaries attached to the matrix head, aspect auxiliaries, voiced past, passive negatives, na-adjective copulas, `ずる` conjugation, and productive godan e-row polite potentials. E-row `仮定形`/`命令形` parser labels followed by polite auxiliaries preserve the source stem across positive/negative and present/past forms; they do not become performed actions by substituting the dictionary lemma.

The new evidence operator moves the already-requested action before its full literal object. All three component witnesses are required: the yorusama right-dislocation witness and both nega request-question witnesses. A restricted series cannot borrow the absent component. Speaker identity is not certified, and multiple examples are not independent human validation.

## Verification remains restrictive

The independent verifier still does not import the planner. It re-recognizes relations against immutable source IR and regenerates the registered realization, then checks exact relation/metadata equality, full source-slot coverage, per-role quantities, evidence identity/text hashes, source provenance, local-edit permissions, schema versions, program hash, locked replay and rendered output spans.

Literal slots cannot receive lexical or grammar edits. Embedded scopes cannot be rewritten by borrowing permission from their matrix clause. New event-binding and evidence-request metadata are source-rebound; rehashing forged fields cannot authorize them. Independent request conditions accidentally attached inside a carrier are refused rather than moved into a mentioned target.

## Known limits

The parser still has unresolved actor, attachment, nominalization and modality cases. Conservative refusals remain, including unresolved generic-noun, genitive and dative assessment ownership, unsupported requested actions, actionless acquisition desires without a preserving structural recipe, unbound carrier/evidence relations, ambiguous request conditions, and purposes that could be mistaken for causes. Lower-ichidan morphology alone does not distinguish every lexical potential from an ordinary verb, so those events retain an explicitly unresolved modality classification.

Structural recognition, planned candidates, independently passed candidates, measured body composition and displayed candidates must be reported separately. Neither a structural label nor a passed meaning-preservation proof establishes style quality. S/Q remain null; structural priority is unchanged and C is considered later.

## Validation scope and repaired regressions

The aggregate suite and independent review are separate from the finite operator proof. They uncovered defects that smaller passing suites had missed: a global quotation-marker change damaged reported-speaker attribution; a report-verb matrix guard masked an assessment-ownership false positive; and an older evidence-question path dropped requested presentation actions. The quotation behavior was restored, report-complement fallback now uses the event's own source span, evaluator ownership is checked per head, and mixed existence-question syntax is restricted to actual source existence questions. Positive presentation/inspection wishes and benefactive requests use action-preserving carrier roles; a desiderative question such as `見せてほしい？` is refused because its requester role can differ.

Some earlier expected refusals were deliberately replaced with stronger source-preservation tests: full literal compound connectives can remain in carrier context; an uncertain mentioned target can remain literal; and a source past event need not be a successful achievement. Schema-version, quantity, forged-role, attribution, polarity, actor and replay checks remain required. B2 source and evaluations are preserved separately.

The final bounded correction also treats matrix explanatory/nominal negation as a relation-wide scope: `AのでBのではない` must not be decomposed into an asserted reason and plain negative B. That unresolved sentence remains literal inside structural programs, including their local-edit layer; ordinary predicate negation and a negative proposition inside the reason retain separate supported paths. A carrier request owns only its current source sentence, so an independently transformable preceding reason or assessment keeps its own block rather than becoming swallowed context.

## Long-input candidate budget

For inputs above 2,000 Unicode scalars, the structural planner emits at most two distinct programs: its first grammar-only composition and a source-lexical companion when a retained lexical variant is available. Per-block rotation still permits multiple operator types within that document. Smaller inputs retain the existing full search. This deliberately reduces long-input choices to reserve time for source analysis and independent validation under the unchanged 30-second API deadline. It does not truncate source text, raise deadlines, change ranking, cache verifier decisions, or bypass any proof. The serialized 12-plan search ceiling remains the global upper bound, not a promise to exhaust every variant on every input.
